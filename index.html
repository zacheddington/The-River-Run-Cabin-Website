About the Property

Nestled right on the banks of Henry's Fork of the Snake River, The River Run Cabin offers a true angler's paradise and a peaceful retreat for nature lovers. Step out your door to immediate river access—perfect for fishing, floating, or simply soaking in the tranquil views. The property is just a minute from the main river take-out, making it effortless to enjoy a day on the water. After your adventures, store your fishing gear and unwind by the cozy fireplace or fire up the grill for a riverside meal. Whether you're seeking world-class fly fishing, a family getaway, or a relaxing escape surrounded by the beauty of Idaho, The River Run Cabin is your home on the river.

Fishing the Henry's Fork

The Henry's Fork is famous for big, wild rainbow trout, and the cabin sits right on its banks. Fish from the property, or launch a drift boat at one of the public access points just up the road.

Stone Bridge (Fisherman's River Access)

5.7 miles · about 16 minutes by car

Head up Fishermans Drive to this Idaho Fish & Game access, with a concrete boat ramp, parking, and open bank to fish. It's day use only (no camping) and has no garbage service, so pack it in and pack it out.

Jump Off Canyon

0.3 miles · about 1 minute by car

Another public access on the Henry's Fork, with a large gravel parking area right at the water. A good spot to put in or take out a float.
